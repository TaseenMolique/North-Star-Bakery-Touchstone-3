Breads

We bake sourdough, whole grain, and other rotating loaves. Most breads are between $5 and $9, depending on the size and ingredients.

Pastries

Our pastry case usually includes croissants, muffins, danishes, and a few seasonal items. Most pastries range from $3 to $6 each.

Cakes

We make simple cakes for birthdays, small celebrations, and other events. Most cakes range from $30 to $65. Customers can contact us for flavor options and advance ordering.

Pre-Orders

There is no online checkout on this site. To ask about a pre-order, use the form on our Contact page.
