Our Story

North Star Bakery started as a small local shop with one goal: make good baked food that people can enjoy without making things complicated. Over time, regular customers helped the bakery become part of the neighborhood.

How We Choose Ingredients

We try to use dependable ingredients and buy locally when it makes sense. Our menu stays fairly simple, and seasonal ingredients are used for some pastries and cake flavors during the year.

Meet the Team

Our staff includes bakers who arrive early to prepare the day's bread, counter staff who help customers, and a small cake team that handles pre-orders. Everyone works together to keep the shop friendly and organized.
