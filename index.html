Welcome

North Star Bakery is a small neighborhood bakery that makes fresh bread and pastries each morning. We want people to have a simple place to stop for breakfast, pick up bread, or order something for a special event.

What We're Known For

Our most popular items are crusty breads, morning pastries, and made-to-order cakes. We make our products in small batches so they are fresh when customers come in.

Featured Item of the Week

This week's feature is a fresh seasonal pastry. Quantities are limited each day, so customers can contact us ahead of time if they want one set aside.
